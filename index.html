A LITTLE HELP

From spreadsheet to inventory.

1. Upload your list

Choose a worksheet and the row containing your column headings. Codes and barcodes are kept as text.

2. Match your fields

Review automatic matches and set company, categories, or POS defaults. Empty columns are left out.

3. Test in Odoo

Download Excel or CSV, then use Odoo’s Test button before importing. Preserve External IDs when updating products.
